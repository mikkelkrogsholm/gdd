# Invoice display request

## Per-line amounts

Support asked to display rounded amounts for individual lines. Changing what customers see and changing what they pay are separate decisions. The [rounding question](../gdd-graph.md#gdd-question-question.rounding) tracks the choice. The published [calculation](../gdd-graph.md#gdd-node-action.total) remains the reference for the current invoice.
